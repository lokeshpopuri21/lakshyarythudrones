Founder's Story June 2026 · 4 min read

From a Lecture Hall to the Skies — How Lokesh Popuri Built Andhra Pradesh's First One-Stop Drone Store

It was during a technical seminar on drone architecture at JNTU Kakinada that Lokesh Popuri — then a young engineer — looked at the projected cutaway of an unmanned aircraft and had a thought that would change everything: "Why is there no one in Andhra Pradesh who can sell you a drone, supply genuine spare parts, fix it when it breaks, and train you to fly it — all in one place?"

That question became Lakshya Aerotech.

"I didn't just want to sell drones. I wanted to build India's most complete drone ecosystem — where every customer, from a farmer in Guntur to a logistics company in Vijayawada, could find exactly what they need." — Lokesh Popuri, Founder & CEO

The Problem He Set Out to Solve

When Lokesh surveyed the market, he found a fragmented landscape. Farmers bought agri drones from one vendor, sourced Hobbywing motors elsewhere, hunted for genuine propellers from a third supplier, and travelled to another city for repairs. There was no single, trusted partner.

Building the Store from the Ground Up

With deep hands-on expertise — from V9 flight controllers and JYI K3-A Pro systems to Hobbywing X8 motors, GPS modules, spray booms, and ESCs — Lokesh didn't just understand drones theoretically. He stocked them, tested them, and built relationships with top OEM suppliers to ensure every part sold at Lakshya Aerotech is 100% genuine. Today the store serves farmers, businesses, research institutions, and defence contractors across 20+ states.
